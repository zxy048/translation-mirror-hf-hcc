# Highlights

- Conserved translational co-expression programs span heart failure and liver cancer.
- 24 of 33 translation pathways show opposite perturbation directions across diseases.
- Mirror regulation is a descriptive cross-disease framework, not a causal mechanism.
- ATF4/ISR and MYC programs correlate with translational remodeling in HCC.
- Conserved molecular organization does not imply universal prognostic utility.
